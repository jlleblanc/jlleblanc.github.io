### How We Can Work Together

I partner with clients in three primary ways:

- **Brunch Installations:** I offer full installation and custom configuration
  of Brunch to match your specific needs, so you own your platform without the
  monthly "SaaS" bloat.
- **Extended Engagements:** Ongoing technical leadership and development. I act
  as your "on-call" CTO to build out your roadmap and manage your tech stack.
- **One-off Assessments & Projects:** Need a second opinion on your current
  setup? A security audit? Technical Due Diligence? I’m available for targeted,
  high-impact projects.
